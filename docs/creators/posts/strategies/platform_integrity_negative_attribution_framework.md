# Platform Integrity, Negative Attribution & Anti-Gaming Framework

## Core Principle

Not all engagement is positive value.

Engagement systems naturally optimize toward whatever generates the strongest measurable signals. Without safeguards, platforms drift toward:

- outrage
- polarization
- pornography bait
- addictive doomscrolling
- misinformation
- spam
- engagement farming
- bot amplification

A sustainable creator economy must optimize for:

- ecosystem value
- healthy participation
- originality
- trust
- educational utility
- meaningful communities

Not merely raw engagement volume.

---

# I. Negative Attribution System

In addition to positive earning events:

```text

```

The platform must maintain:


- reduce payouts

# XVIII. Strategic Principles

1. Healthy ecosystems outperform addictive ecosystems long-term.
2. Originality should outperform aggregation.
3. Trust should outweigh volume.
4. Quality engagement should outweigh manipulation.
5. Transparent attribution builds creator trust.
6. Recommendation systems must resist outrage optimization.
7. Sustainable monetization requires integrity enforcement.
8. Platform incentives shape platform culture.
9. Safety systems must exist before monetization scales.
10. Ecosystem health is a first-class economic metric.
